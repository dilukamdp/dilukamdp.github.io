Dr. Diluka Moratuwage is a Lecturer in Industrial Automation at the School of Engineering 
and Technology, Central Queensland University (CQU), based at the Gladstone Marina campus. 
He obtained his PhD in Mobile Robotics from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, 
and his B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.

His research spans artificial intelligence, multi-robot coordination, mobile robot navigation, 
multi-robot SLAM, multi-target tracking and sensor fusion, and computer vision. He is currently 
exploring multi-agent reinforcement learning, drone-based localisation systems, and robotic 
automation for environmental monitoring applications.

## Career History

Prior to joining CQU, Dr. Moratuwage held research positions at:

- **University of Technology Sydney (UTS)** — Research Fellow (2023–2024)
- **Curtin University** — Research Associate & Associate Lecturer (2019–2022)
- **Universidad de Chile** — Postdoctoral Researcher (2018–2019)
- **Nanyang Technological University** — Postdoctoral Researcher (2015–2018)
- **Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology (SMART) Centre** — Autonomous Vehicle 
  Technologies (2013–2015)
